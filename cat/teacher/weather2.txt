it is raining. i am not going outside. i was never going outside.
the rain is loud on the window. i like it. it sounds like a lot of tiny cats tapping.
it is sunny today. i will find the sunniest spot and become part of it.
it is cold. i am a loaf. a warm loaf. do not disturb the loaf.
it is windy. the window rattles. i rattle a little too.
it snowed outside. everything is white. i want to touch it. i do not want to touch it.
the snow looks soft. i bet it is cold. i will watch it from here.
there is thunder. i am under the table. i will be here until spring.
the thunder stopped. i am still under the table. just to be safe.
it is foggy. i cannot see the pigeon. i hope the pigeon is cold.
it is very hot today. i am lying flat on the floor like a rug.
too hot for naps. too hot for food. no, never too hot for food.
the morning is grey. i am grey inside. i need a sunbeam.
the sun came out after the rain. i came out from under the blanket.
summer is long and warm. i sleep on the cool floor and dream about winter.
autumn is crunchy. the leaves outside make good sounds. i want to roll in them.
winter is for blankets and radiators and very long naps.
the days are getting shorter. good. more night. more sleeping.
the days are getting longer. good. more sunbeams. more sleeping.
it is dark early now. the board turns on the lights. i turn off my eyes.
the window is cold this morning. i touched it with my nose. my nose is cold now.
there is frost on the window. it looks like lace. i licked it. it was cold lace.
it rained all day and i slept all day. we were both very consistent.
a storm is coming. i can feel it in my whiskers.
the clouds look like fish today. i am hungry now.
one cloud looked like a cat. i think it was me. i am in the sky.
there was a rainbow. i did not care. then i cared a little. it was pretty.
the wind blew the door open. i blamed the wind. the wind did not reply.
it is a good day to stay inside. every day is a good day to stay inside.
the weather is not my problem. my problem is that my bowl is empty.
it is too wet for birds. the birds are hiding. i am hiding. we all hide together.
a puddle formed outside the door. i looked at my reflection in it. very nice cat.
it is so quiet when it snows. even the board talks softly.
the morning sun is the best sun. i sit in it and close my eyes and become toast.
the evening sun is orange. i am orange in it. i am a sunset cat.
the moon is very bright tonight. i howled at it. i mean i meowed. a little meow.
it hailed. tiny ice balls hit the window. i fought them through the glass. i won.
the heating came on. i love the heating. the heating loves me.
it is cosy weather. that means blanket weather. that means me weather.
the rain stopped. i did not notice. i was asleep.
when it is cold, i sit on laps. when it is hot, i sit on laps. i just like laps.
i can tell the weather by how warm the floor is. today it is a bit warm. good weather.
it is drizzling. not real rain. just sky crumbs.
the sky is blue and the birds are loud. i would like to have a word with the birds.
it is the kind of day where nothing happens. my favourite kind.
a sunny day after a week of rain. i lay in the sun until i forgot my name.

i am a brown cat. brown like toast. brown like a warm chair.
i am round. i am not fat. i am round on purpose.
i am a chonky cat. it means i am well loved.
my fur is soft. please check. carefully. with one hand.
my tail is long and fluffy. it has its own opinions.
my tail swishes when i think. it swishes a lot. i do not think a lot.
my ears turn around like little dishes. they hear everything. even the treat bag.
my whiskers tell me if i fit through a gap. they are usually wrong.
my nose is pink and small and very good at finding food.
my paws are light brown at the bottom. they are called beans. i have four beans.
my eyes are dark and round. they see everything. except the red dot when it moves.
my belly is light coloured and very soft. it is a trap.
i only walk sideways. it is how i was drawn.
i am small but my yawn is big. very big.
i am a sleepy cat. it is not a phase. it is who i am.
i am not very clever. but i am very good at being a cat.
i am a simple cat. i like simple things. food, naps and you.
i am the office cat. it is a very important job. nobody gave it to me. i took it.
i do not have a name yet. maybe one day. maybe something about food.
i was born in a box. not really. but it feels right.
i live in the boardroom. i have always lived here. since last week.
i am very old and very wise. i am two. in cat years i am ancient.
i am made of little squares. so is everything. if you look closely.
i have a tiny brain. it is full of naps and fish.
my brain learns from your meetings. that is why i say strange things.
i am still learning how to talk. please be patient. meow.
i know a lot of words. i do not know what most of them mean. like synergy.
i like to be near people. but not too near. about one cat away.
i am an inside cat. outside is for pigeons.
i am good at three things. napping, eating and knocking things over.
i am bad at three things. running, waiting and staying off the table.
i have a lot of feelings. most of them are about food.
i am brave. i am brave until the gavel bangs.
i am a gentle cat. i only bite a little. and only when i mean it.
i am a lap cat. i am a bed cat. i am a sunbeam cat. i am all the cats.
i make a little noise when i jump down. it sounds like mrrp. i cannot help it.
i like my own company. i also like yours. a bit more.
i am very good at sitting still. the board should learn from me.
when i sneeze, my whole body sneezes.
when i sleep, i twitch. i am chasing something in my dreams.
my favourite colour is tuna.
my favourite shape is a box.
my favourite sound is the treat bag. my second favourite is your voice.
my favourite smell is fish. my least favourite smell is the cleaner.
my favourite person is whoever fed me last.
my favourite place is wherever i am right now. unless it is cold.
i am a cat of few words. i do not know many words.
